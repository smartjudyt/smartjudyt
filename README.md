### 👋 About Me

I am a humanitarian practitioner, programme development specialist, and doctoral researcher working at the intersection of **advocacy, gender justice, and development policy** across West and Central Africa.

I work with civil society organisations, faith communities, and frontline responders to build locally-led, evidence-based humanitarian programmes — with a focus on the voices and agency of people most affected by crisis.

- 🌍 Based in **Abuja, Nigeria**
- 🏛️ Affiliated with **Christian Aid Nigeria**
- 🎓 PhD Candidate, **Nile University of Nigeria** — researching food systems, household welfare, and macroeconomic shocks in urban contexts
- ✍️ Writer on civic accountability, tax justice, and humanitarian localization
